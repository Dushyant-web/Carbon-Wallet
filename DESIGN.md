## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, data-dense, and highly legible visual language tailored for institutional carbon auditing, household energy intelligence, and ESG portfolio management. It merges modern enterprise SaaS utility with the disciplined precision of institutional fintech terminals. 

The emotional response must convey rigorous accounting precision, actionable optimism, and immediate situational clarity. Rather than relying on ornamental environmental tropes, the aesthetic leverages crisp high-contrast information hierarchies, dense tabular surfaces, and strict structural boundaries. 

The aesthetic style is **Corporate Modern with High-Density Precision**:
- Surgical white and cool slate foundations with low-noise structural boundaries.
- Strategic ecological chromatic accents (deep forest emeralds and mints) reserved for positive carbon offsets, operational targets, and actionable insights.
- Restrained, unambiguous semantic signaling for anomalous consumption surges and compliance deadlines.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast tiers designed for dense telemetry visualizations, complex data grids, and rapid scanning.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#064E3B` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, active enterprise workspace switchers, high-level reporting headers, and key navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Emerald Accent):** Expresses net-zero milestone achievements, positive reduction vectors, verified renewable feeds, and interactive micro-highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#34D399` - Mint Soft):** Used for subtle vector gradients, interactive focus rings, secondary telemetry series, and high-visibility data sparklines against dark fills.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate Navy):** The structural ink. Dictates primary typography, dark navigation bars, iconography, and maximum-contrast structural frames.

### Supporting Canvas & Semantic Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) provides an anti-glare, pristine field for extended operational monitoring.
- **Surface Card:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) defines floating intelligence modules, telemetry pods, and active data rows.
- **Structural Border:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) governs all 1px spatial dividers, data grids, and component enclosures.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B` - Carbon Alert Amber):** Warns against projected carbon threshold overages and seasonal grid spikes.
- **Critical (`#EF4444` - High Emission Crimson):** Demands immediate remediation for anomaly surges, fossil fuel baseline spikes, and audit compliance failures.
- **Muted Text:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) for metadata, timestamp strings, units of measurement, and table column headers.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural authority and high-impact numerical dashboards with **Inter** for dense tabular, analytical, and narrative contexts.

### Tabular Figures & Numerical Standards
All numeric values—such as metric readings (MT CO₂e), kilowatt-hours (kWh), currency offsets, and percentage deltas—must explicitly enable OpenType tabular figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv02" 1, "cv03" 1, "cv04" 1`). This prevents column jitter in real-time telemetry tables and comparative cards.

### Scaling and Readability Rules
- Use `display-lg` exclusively for top-level aggregation values (e.g., total household portfolio footprint).
- Use `label-sm` with uppercase transformation and `0.04em` letter spacing for metric descriptors, table sorting heads, and micro status chips.
- Maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 7:1 for all primary analytical body text against white and slate surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an uninterrupted **Fluid 12-Column Responsive Grid** engineered for data-dense dashboards, analytical split-panes, and high-frequency metric cards.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of `2rem` (`32px`). Maximum application canvas caps at 1920px for multi-monitor command setups.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins. Sidebar collapses to an icon rail to prioritize real-time chart widths.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) canvas margins. Side-by-side metric tiles collapse into single-column vertical stacks.

### Spacing Principles
- Internal card padding defaults strictly to `space-lg` (`24px`) on desktop and `space-md` (`16px`) on mobile.
- Use `space-xs` (`4px`) for tight pairings between units and metric values (e.g., `412` and `tCO₂e`).
- Use `space-sm` (`8px`) for stack gaps between micro labels and main headline KPIs.
- Preserve vertical rhythmic consistency using strict multiples of `4px` (`0.25rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep, decorative drop shadows in favor of a crisp **Low-Contrast Outlines & Precise Ambient Tiers** model. Structural separation is primarily achieved through hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle background shifts.

### Elevation Scales
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Surface color `#F8FAFC`. Completely flat. Used as the underlying framework for all views.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Surface color `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for metric cards, table containers, and filter toolbars.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover):** Surface color `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and an elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Applied to active dropdown menus, date pickers, popovers, and interactive card hovers.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Critical Drawers):** Surface color `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, framed by a crisp border (`#E2E8F0`). Underlaid by a slate backdrop scrim (`#0F172A` at 40% opacity with a `2px` blur).

### Accent Outlines
Data states use a distinct focused boundary: interactive elements gain a 2px offset focus ring using Tertiary `#34D399` to ensure accessible navigation across high-density grids.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`level 1`)** geometric language. This subtle curvature maintains the disciplined, institutional credibility of a terminal while avoiding the abrasive harshness of sharp zero-radius geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Applied to data chips, status badges, contextual tags, input elements, checkboxes, table cell hover targets, and pagination items.
- **Card Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px` - `rounded-lg`):** Standard for telemetry cards, analytical chart panels, sub-navigation tabs, and modal window containers.
- **Container Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px` - `rounded-xl`):** Reserved for primary application canvas wrappers, floating command overlays, and large multi-metric summary banners.
- **Full Radius (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for numerical indicator pills, live operational pings, and user identity avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep forest green (`#064E3B`) fill, white (`#FFFFFF`) text, subtle hover shift to `#043A2C`. Focus state yields a `2px` offset ring in Mint (`#34D399`). Height: 36px (compact default) or 40px (prominent).
- **Secondary:** White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions border to `#CBD5E1` with a `#F8FAFC` background.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or fill. Slate text (`#64748B`), hover to text `#0F172A` with `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Destructive:** Crimson (`#EF4444`) fill, white text, hover to `#DC2626`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Metric Chips:** High-contrast, tabular typography. Format: 11px uppercase bold (`label-sm`).
  - *Positive/Offset:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *Warning/Spike:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`.
  - *High Emission/Alert:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`.
  - *Neutral/Benchmark:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `#E2E8F0`.

### Data Cards
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background with a continuous 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Layout: Top rail consists of metric label (`label-sm`, Slate 500) and contextual action icon. Body contains the large numerical KPI (`kpi-metric`, `#0F172A`, tabular numbers). Footer features the comparison delta chip accompanied by a micro sparkline or secondary benchmark line.

### Lists & Data Grids
- **Header Row:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase 11px tracking text (`#64748B`), bottom border 1px `#E2E8F0`.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#FFFFFF`, height 44px (high-density mode) or 56px (standard mode). Row hover states shift cleanly to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Dividers:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0` with no vertical cell lines to maintain horizontal reading speed.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** 36px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Active focus transitions border to `#064E3B` and applies a 2px mint glow (`rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`). Checked state fills with `#064E3B` and displays a crisp white checkmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Household Portfolio Switcher:** Multi-tenant selector element displaying household alias, active emission band color badge, and sync status indicator.
- **Emission Factor Indicator:** Inline component combining a dot indicator (green/amber/crimson) with real-time grid carbon intensity (`gCO₂/kWh`).
- **Telemetry Sparkline:** Frameless SVGs integrated within cards; positive reduction trends use `#10B981` (Emerald), while baseline spikes render in `#EF4444` (Crimson).
